# Emergence (OpenGL) - Kodi screensaver

Physarum v2, Dancing Boids and Particle Life v2 in one world: seven scenes that show each alone or
couple them through the slime's trail map. See the "Emergence (OpenGL)" section of the repository
README for the scenes, settings and measurements.

- `src/Physarum.h`, `src/Boids.h`, `src/ParticleLife.h` - the three simulation cores, copied from
  their add-ons with one hook each: `Simulation::Deposit()` lays scent on the slime's trail map,
  `boids::Field` lets the flock steer up or down an 8-bit map, and
  `particlelife::Simulation::StepWith()` splits each group pair over the worker pool.
- `src/PhysarumPresets.h`, `src/ParticleLifePresets.h` - the presets and palettes of the two.
- `src/World.h` - the scenes and couplings (no Kodi / GL): which simulations run, how big each is
  per detail level, the particles and boids depositing on the trail map, the particles' density
  map for Reef, and one `Step()` that runs everything over the pool.
- `src/WorkerPool.h` - the fork-join pool from Dancing Boids.
- `src/ScreensaverEmergence.cpp` - the Kodi wrapper: settings, a driver thread that steps the
  world (and changes scene behind a fade) while Kodi draws, step credit at 30/60/120 steps a
  second, one texture upload and one vertex stream per step; the slime (or Reef's density glow)
  as one quad, the particles as points and the boids as triangles.
- `tests/sim_test.cpp` - the parallel Particle Life step against the serial one, field steering,
  every scene at every detail level, the couplings (the slime gathers on the food, the flock keeps
  out of the cells), benchmarks; `DIR` writes a preview picture of each scene.

    c++ -O3 -std=c++17 -Isrc tests/sim_test.cpp -o /tmp/emergence_test -pthread && /tmp/emergence_test
    ./build_all.sh    # macOS universal dylib, arm64 .so, and ../screensaver.emergence.armv7 .so

Settings are generated by `../tools/make_settings_emergence.py` (both add-ons); the option indices
there match the enums and lists in `ScreensaverEmergence.cpp` and `World.h`.
