Bundled media
-------------

resources/media/icons
  Meteocons by Bas Milius (MIT), github.com/basmilius/weather-icons, rendered to PNG.

resources/media/glyphs
  Digits rendered from Inter by Rasmus Andersson (SIL Open Font License 1.1), rsms.me/inter.

resources/media/moon
  From God's Eye Map in this repository (MIT).

Full licence texts are in resources/media/LICENSES.txt.

Data is fetched at run time from public services under their own terms (Open-Meteo,
Iowa Environmental Mesonet, NOAA/NWS, NASA GIBS, USGS, RainViewer, CARTO, Esri, and the
optional Rainbow.ai, Stadia Maps and LibreWXR). See the add-on description for credits.
