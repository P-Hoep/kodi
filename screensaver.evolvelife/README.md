# EvolveLife (OpenGL) - Kodi screensaver

Glowing creatures evolve their bodies and brains from scratch to travel, reach beacons, climb,
cross chasms, leap and glide, while the screen tells the story of the run. Built from the
specification in [`../EvolveLife.md`](../EvolveLife.md); see the "EvolveLife (OpenGL)" section of
the repository README for what it does, the settings and the measurements.

- `src/Common.h` - deterministic RNG, hashing, fast atan2 / tanh, value noise.
- `src/World.h` - goals (travel, beacon, climb, chasm, leap, glide, hurdles) and seeded terrain
  heightfields within solvable bounds.
- `src/Body.h` - the morphology genome (node/edge graph with repeats, mirroring and recursion),
  repair, expansion into segments and joints, mutation, crossover, descriptors.
- `src/Brain.h` - NEAT: innovation record, genomes, mutation, crossover, compatibility distance,
  and the runtime network (fixed-size interface: 16 joint slots, 8 contact slots, clocks).
- `src/Trial.h` - one creature in its own world: Verlet point masses, rods, momentum-conserving
  motor joints (position-based angle constraints), Coulomb ground contact, fin drag, the
  controller at 30 Hz, goal scoring and the anti-exploit rules.
- `src/Evolution.h` - population, speciation, fitness sharing, breeding, immigrants, epochs with
  random goals, MAP-Elites archive, telemetry, milestones, hall of fame; publishes snapshots.
- `src/Stage.h`, `src/Draw.h` - the live replay, camera Director and the insight layer (title,
  ticker, sparklines, live brain, info cards) as quad batches; `src/Font.h` is the baked font.
- `src/ScreensaverEvolveLife.cpp` - the Kodi shell: settings, the evolver and driver threads,
  quality governor, GL.
- `src/WorkerPool.h` - the fork-join pool from Dancing Boids, plus dynamic hand-out and
  low-priority workers.
- `tests/sim_test.cpp` - body fuzzing, physics stability and momentum, determinism (threads and
  replays), evolution regression on every goal, benchmarks.

    c++ -O3 -std=c++17 -Isrc tests/sim_test.cpp -o /tmp/evolve_test -pthread && /tmp/evolve_test
    /tmp/evolve_test evolve 0 40      # watch one run's progress (goal 0-6, generations)
    ./build_all.sh                    # macOS universal dylib, arm64 .so, ../screensaver.evolvelife.armv7 .so

Settings are generated by `../tools/make_settings_evolvelife.py` (both add-ons); the font by
`../tools/make_evolvelife_font.py`.
