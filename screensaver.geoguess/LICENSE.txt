The add-on bundles no images. Street view panoramas are fetched at run time from the Hugging
Face dataset "yunusserhat/random_streetview_images" through the datasets server, with
"stochastic/random_streetview_images_pano_v0.0.2" as a fallback. The imagery is © Google and
its contributors and is subject to Google's terms of use; this add-on is not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by Google. The place names come from the same dataset.
