The add-on bundles no photographs and no map image. Ship positions come from aisstream.io and
are used under that service's terms; an API key from aisstream.io is required and is stored in
the add-on settings. The map is NASA Blue Marble imagery rendered by NASA EOSDIS GIBS, which is
in the public domain. Ship details are read from Wikidata (CC0) and photographs are fetched at
run time from Wikimedia Commons, where each file keeps its own licence and attribution; they
are cached in the add-on profile and are not redistributed with this add-on.
