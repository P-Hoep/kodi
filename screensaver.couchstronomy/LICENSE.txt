resources/data/vsop87.json
  Truncated VSOP87B series (P. Bretagnon and G. Francou, Bureau des Longitudes, 1988),
  taken from the astronomia library (github.com/commenthol/astronomia),

  Lunar series from J. Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms (2nd ed.), chapter 47, as
  transcribed in the astronomia library (MIT License, see above).

  Rendered from the CGI Moon Kit colour map (Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter LROC), NASA's
