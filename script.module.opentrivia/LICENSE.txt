(https://github.com/uberspot/OpenTriviaQA), licensed under the Creative Commons

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made by tools/make_opentrivia_db.py: the category files were parsed; text was
normalised (Unicode, HTML entities, punctuation, whitespace); duplicate questions were
removed; questions longer than 420 characters were dropped; answers were reduced to at most
four choices and shuffled; the rows were stored in random order in SQLite.

question data.
