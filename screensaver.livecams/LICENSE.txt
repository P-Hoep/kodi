Camera data and feeds
---------------------

The camera list is not bundled. The add-on downloads streams.geojson and sources.json from
https://github.com/william-ricchiuti/Live-Environment-Streams at run time; the dataset has
no licence of its own yet. Each camera feed belongs to its operator, whose name is shown on
screen. The add-on plays the public streams as published, records nothing and sends no
telemetry.

Bundled media
-------------

icon.png, fanart.jpg and resources/media/* are drawn by tools/make_livecams_media.py and
tools/make_splash.py in this repository (MIT, as above).
